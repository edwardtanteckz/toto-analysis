Singapore Pools TOTO & 4D: is it random?

Every draw is independent. These charts test whether the past results are consistent with fair, uniform draws. They can't predict future numbers. Grey bands and dashed lines show what 10,000 simulated fair histories look like (Monte Carlo baseline).
